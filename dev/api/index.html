API

Registration

PanoramaStitching.register_rotations — Function
register_rotations(frames, camera; raw_scale, circle, tone_percentile, detector_margin_px, asift_tilts, max_detections,
                   descriptor, matcher, min_score, pairs, max_lag, overlap_deg, min_inliers, seed,
                   scoring = nothing, local_optimization = nothing, max_trials, min_trials,
                   averaging_loss, bundle_loss, bundle_scale_multiple, bundle_iterations, bundle_max_iter, bundle_ftol,
                   bundle_xtol, say = _ -> nothing) -> Registration

The rotations of the pure-rotation sequence frames (H × W × 3 linear images in capture order, raw_scale camera pixels per frame pixel) seen by the calibrated camera (fixed; any camera with backproject_with_jacobian, backproject and project), with the view graph, the tracks and the bundle's statistics. Every setting is the caller's configuration, none defaulted but the RANSAC's:

  • features: circle = (cx, cy, r) (camera pixels), tone_percentile, detector_margin_px, asift_tilts (nothing: IMAS-25), max_detections — see detect_features; descriptor and matcher, VisualGeometryToolkitFeatures' LoMaDescriptor and LoMaMatcher variants (:b, :b128); min_score.
  • pairs: pairs = :all (every frame pair) or :lag_overlap (pairs at most max_lag apart, then those whose averaged optical axes are within overlap_deg); a pair is verified by find_rotation (its scoring and local_optimization objects, nothing for VisualGeometryToolkitEstimation's defaults; max_trials, min_trials; the sampler seeded from seed) and kept with at least min_inliers inliers.
  • graph and bundle: averaging_loss, bundle_loss (stack loss shapes), bundle_scale_multiple, bundle_iterations, bundle_max_iter, bundle_ftol, bundle_xtol — see refine_rotations.

say receives one line per frame, pair and step.

PanoramaStitching.Registration — Type
Registration{T}

The result of register_rotations: rotations (one RotMatrix{3} per frame, d = R_k·f_k, the first frame's camera frame the common frame), graph (the ViewGraph of the verified pairs), pairs (per verified pair (i, j): matches, inliers, the rms Sampson distance of its inliers in pixels and its rotation angle in degrees), averaging (the fit_rotation_averaging result), tracks (the tracks as (frame, keypoint) observations), dropped (inconsistent tracks dropped), bundle (the refine_rotations result) and features (the frames' FrameFeatures).

source
PanoramaStitching.ViewGraph — Type
ViewGraph{K}

The view graph of a sequence: graph (a Graphs.SimpleGraph on the frames, vertex v the frame frames[v]), and per verified pair (i, j) (frame keys, i < j in frames order) its relative rotation rotations[(i, j)] (f_j = R_ij·f_i) and inlier count inliers[(i, j)].

PanoramaStitching.chain_tracks — Function
chain_tracks(matches) -> (tracks, n_dropped)

The tracks of a sequence: the connected sets of observations (frame, keypoint) through every pair's inlier matches, matches an AbstractDict keyed by frame pairs (i, j) whose value is a vector of keypoint index pairs (a, b) (keypoint a of frame i ↔ keypoint b of frame j). Union-find over the observations (DataStructures.jl's IntDisjointSets). A track that holds two different keypoints of one frame is inconsistent (a wrong match chained through the graph) and is DROPPED whole; their count is n_dropped. Every track returned has observations in at least two frames, sorted by frame then keypoint.

PanoramaStitching.refine_rotations — Function
refine_rotations(camera, rotations, tracks; loss, scale_multiple, iterations, max_iter, ftol, xtol, eliminate = true)
    -> FitResult{Dict{K, RotMatrix{3, Float64}}, RotationBundleMetadata}

The rotation bundle of a pure-rotation sequence seen by the calibrated camera (any camera with project(camera, f; init) and backproject_with_jacobian, fixed): the rotations R_k (d = R_k·f_k) of every frame but the first of the sorted keys of rotations (the gauge, held fixed) and one direction d_j per track minimizing the robust sum of the pixel reprojection errors π(R_kᵀ d_j) − x_jk of every observation. A refine: it starts from rotations (a rotation averaging of the view graph, VisualGeometryToolkitEstimation.fit_rotation_averaging) and each track's mean bearing in the common frame.

  • tracks — AbstractVector of tracks, each an AbstractVector of frame => pixel observations (pixels in the camera's frame), two frames at least.
  • loss, scale_multiple — the stack loss shape (NLLSsolver's robustifier, loss_robustifier) and its width as a multiple of the Rayleigh scale of the reprojection error norms at the start of each of the iterations NLLSsolver runs (max_iter, ftol, xtol each: nlls_options).
  • eliminate — whether NLLSsolver may eliminate the directions (the Schur complement, where the loaded NLLSsolver has it); false solves every unknown together. Both reach the same optimum.

PanoramaStitching.RotationBundleMetadata — Type
RotationBundleMetadata

FitResult metadata of refine_rotations: directions (one unit direction per track, the common frame), residuals (per track, the pixel reprojection error norm of each of its observations at the result), loss_width (px, of the last NLLSsolver run), n_observations.

PanoramaStitching.FrameFeatures — Type
FrameFeatures

One frame's features: pixels (the kept detections' centres in the camera's pixel frame, full resolution), features (their DeDoDe descriptions), n_detected (ASIFT's count before the circle and the strength selection).

PanoramaStitching.detect_features — Function
detect_features(frame, descriptor; raw_scale, circle, tone_percentile, detector_margin_px, asift_tilts, max_detections)
    -> FrameFeatures

The features of one frame (H × W × 3 linear, raw_scale camera pixels per frame pixel): ASIFT on sqrt(G / p) clamped to [0, 1] (p the tone_percentile-th percentile of the green channel inside the image circle = (cx, cy, r), camera pixels), the detections inside the circle less detector_margin_px, the max_detections strongest by |response|, described by descriptor (a LoMaDescriptor) on the frame's tone-mapped RGB resampled to the network's input. asift_tilts is VisualGeometryToolkitFeatures' tilt schedule (nothing: IMAS-25).

PanoramaStitching.pair_bearings — Function
pair_bearings(camera, fa, fb, matches) -> (cs, kept)

The matches of two frames as unit-bearing correspondences with their back-projection Jacobians as the M₁/M₂ columns and the match score as the :score column (VisualGeometryToolkitCore's backproject_with_jacobian through the calibrated camera: the whole field, past 90°), and the keypoint index pairs kept (a pixel the camera cannot back-project is dropped).

Cells and the panorama

PanoramaStitching.world_cells — Function
world_cells(cell_deg) -> Matrix{Float64} (N × 4)

The near-equal-area latitude/longitude grid of the sphere: latitude bands of cell_deg degrees from −90°, each split into max(1, round(360 cos(lat)/cell_deg)) equal longitude cells from −180°. Row i is (lat_lo, lat_hi, lon_lo, lon_hi) in degrees.

PanoramaStitching.CellSamples — Type
CellSamples

The samples of the world cells in every frame: cells (N × 4, world_cells), val (K × N × 3: the mean value per channel), theta (K × N: the recorded field angle of the cell, degrees), theta_model (K × N: the lens's field angle — the mean angle of the cell's sample directions to the optical axis, degrees), px (K × N × 2: the mean pixel, camera pixels), ok (K × N: the frame saw the whole cell).

PanoramaStitching.sample_cells — Function
sample_cells(frames, saturated, rotations, camera; raw_scale, circle, cell_deg, sub, blur_px, rim_margin_px, fov_deg,
             field_angle, f_px = nothing, rng, say = _ -> nothing) -> CellSamples

The world cells of cell_deg sampled in every frame: in every cell sub² sample directions (the cell's sub-grid, each jittered uniformly within its sub-cell from rng, the same in every frame), each taken into frame k (f = R_kᵀ·d, rotations[k] with d = R_k·f) and projected through the calibrated camera (Core's project, a generic model's pixel solve started at its equidistant guess about the circle), sampled bilinearly (Core's bilinear_sample) in the frame smoothed by VisualGeometryToolkitFeatures' masked gaussian_blur (σ blur_px frame pixels, the image circle the mask). A cell is valid in a frame when its centre is within fov_deg of the optical axis (the direction of the circle centre) and every sample direction projects inside the circle = (cx, cy, r) less rim_margin_px, at least 2 px inside the image, onto no saturated pixel and with a positive green value. field_angle = :lens records the lens's angle as theta; :equisolid records 2 asin(ρ / 2 f_px) of the mean pixel's radius ρ about the circle centre (a lens-independent ring convention).

frames are H × W × 3 linear images with raw_scale camera pixels per frame pixel, saturated their H × W masks.

PanoramaStitching.render_panorama — Function
render_panorama(cells, values, ok, gains; up = I) -> (image, extent, composite)

The equirectangular panorama of per-frame cell values, composited in capture order: each cell takes the value of the LAST frame k that saw it (ok[k, i]), divided by that frame's gain exp(gains[k, c]) (values is K × N × C linear, gains K × C in ln; the frames' own corrections, a vignetting for one, are the caller's, applied to values). The raster is in the frame up (d = up·d_up: up_frame for a true-up panorama), its rows the elevation from the top, its columns the azimuth, one pixel per cell size, the nearest world cell (Core's Equirectangular for both directions), spanning the cells seen plus one cell; NaN where no frame saw the cell. extent is (lon₀, lon₁, lat₀, lat₁) in degrees, composite the N × C per-cell values.
